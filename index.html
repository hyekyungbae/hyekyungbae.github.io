About

I am an HCI researcher and educator with a Ph.D. in Human-Centered Computing. My research examines how people experience and interact with emerging technologies, particularly virtual reality. My interests include user experience, usability, virtual reality, information visualization, and instructional technology. Through mixed-methods user studies, my dissertation examined students’ experiences of immersion when using virtual reality for foreign language learning.

My background in instructional systems design and TESOL provides an interdisciplinary foundation for my work, helping me connect human-centered research with the design and evaluation of meaningful, technology-supported immersive experiences.
